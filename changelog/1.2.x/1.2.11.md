# 1.2.11 — 2026-05-31

## Fixed

- **`arxiv_search` / `arxiv_get_metadata` mirror readiness during refresh** — an incremental or failed nightly refresh no longer takes search offline. `tryReadyMirror()` now gates on the durable `completed_at` marker ("a full harvest has ever completed") instead of the live `status === 'complete'`, so the existing complete dataset keeps serving throughout the refresh window rather than falling through to the throttled live API. `writeHarvestState()` preserves `completed_at` and `total_records` via `COALESCE` so they survive `in_progress` and `error` writes. Also fixes the failed-refresh case: a harvest that errors after a prior success keeps the mirror online until the next successful refresh. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/arxiv-mcp-server/issues/21))
